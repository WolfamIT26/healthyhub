# Customer Frontend / Frontend Customer

- `/account` redirect `/account/profile`; direct/reload dùng server state.
- `/account/profile`: skeleton, error/retry, accessible form, read-only email và success toast.
- `/account/addresses`: loading/empty/error, responsive cards, Modal add/edit, default badge/action, ConfirmDialog delete và toast.
- Account navigation dùng shared Design System; form geography là free-text đúng Shipping V1, không hard-code province/district/ward giả.
